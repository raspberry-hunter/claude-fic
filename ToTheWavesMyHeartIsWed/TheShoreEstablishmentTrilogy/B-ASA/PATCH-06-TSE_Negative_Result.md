# Retcon patch: 06-TSE *Negative Result*

**Why.** 07-ASA *Preferment* establishes that Spilsbury stopped it — three seconds, *no, I don't think so*, and then *bring the plate through*. Arc one currently has Reggie say he never knew which of them stopped it, which he would have known perfectly well. The ambiguity that has to survive is not *what happened* but *what Spilsbury understood by it*, because that is the thing 07 pays off.

Nothing else in 06-TSE changes.

---

## Find

> "I've known him eleven years." He says it fast. "We worked in adjoining rooms for four of them. He is the most interesting man I have ever met and I have never been able to read him for a single minute of it, and there was — " A pause of about three seconds. "There was an evening, in 'forty-six, that has never been mentioned by either of us since. Nothing happened. Something very nearly happened. I have never known which of us stopped it and I have never known what he understood by it, and I have arranged eleven years of my life around never finding out."

## Replace with

> "I've known him eleven years." He says it fast. "We worked in adjoining rooms for four of them. He is the most interesting man I have ever met and I have never been able to read him for a single minute of it, and there was — " A pause of about three seconds. "There was an evening, in 'forty-six. In the corridor between the two rooms, at two in the morning. I was twenty-three and I had a plate in my hand and I was going to do something, and he looked at me, and he said *no, I don't think so*. Perfectly pleasantly. And then he asked me to bring the plate through, and I brought the plate through, and we worked until five."
>
> "*Ay.*"
>
> "It has never been mentioned by either of us since and it has never once been awkward, and that is the part I cannot get to the bottom of." Reggie is looking at the floor between his shoes. "I know what happened. What I have never known is what he understood by it — whether he had seen it coming for a year, or whether it landed on him at two in the morning and he dealt with it and forgot it by Thursday. And I have arranged eleven years of my life around never finding out."

## Consequential check

- **05-TSE *Effects***, the Nicholson conversation at the table, is unaffected and improves: Spilsbury's remark now sits on top of a thing Reggie *knows* Spilsbury knows, rather than a thing he hopes was never noticed.
- **07-ASA** now reads as the answer: Spilsbury had added him up before the corridor, kept it eight years, and produced it on a tablecloth in 1954 as a receipt.
- Reggie's line in 06-TSE — *it might have been a threat, or a warning, or an offer, or a man saying an unlucky thing at dinner* — stays exactly as it is. It is still true in 1954. It is simply no longer the last word.
