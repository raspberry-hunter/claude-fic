# The Shore Establishment — Continuity

Everything here is already on the page in installments 1–9, or is a house convention. Nothing forward-looking. Safe to reread.

---

## 1. House rules

- Present tense, close third. POV is **not** restricted to Reggie and Pascual and is not obliged to alternate. Jean, John and Hester are all available and all know different amounts.
- Frontmatter: `title`, `series`, `order`, `status` (`claude-draft` / `my-draft` / `collaboration-draft`), `placement` (date + location + POV), `summary`.
- Word count reported with each file. No summary of the fiction — only notes on what isn't in the text.
- Sex: no explicit gendered body parts; everything else in play; dialogue and feeling do the work.
- Reggie contracts (~5.3/100 words) with rare deliberate spikes; the government-labs content stays off the page permanently.
- Pascual contracts heavily (~5.9/100), never composes in advance, Spanish surfaces as a word or phrase without formalising the English.

**Standing caution.** Both calibration sheets are written as permissions, not prohibitions. Anything that reads like a closed faculty is a description of what a man usually does, or what he believes about himself — not a rule about what he can do.

### Two flags, both fixable, both author's call

- *Waves* gives no date. **May 1954 is invention**, not canon (rains beginning; Fleming already "the writer"). Confirmed acceptable, so locked.
- **"The ninth" is overloaded in installments 2–6.** It means both the date 9 May and the ninth day of the clinical record (= 17 May). The title *The Ninth Day* refers to the record — where the file now stops. Repair is either always writing dates as "the seventeenth" and record days as "day nine," or retitling to *Day Nine*.

## 4. The official story

Beta broke out of the pen in the night of 19/20 May, reached the sea, was not recovered. Charles Cholmondeley went in after him and did not return; presumed drowned. Cause on the certificate: renal failure sequel to the escape. Signed *P. Pobil*. The clinical file's last data entry is 17 May; death note 20 May; a 60-hour gap Spilsbury noticed and named on the jetty.

## 5. Documents

- **The notebook** — c. 400 pp., Charles's hand, back to November 1953. With Hester, hidden where she has kept something for 41 years. She intends it published in ~30 years, under his name, when everyone who could be hurt is dead.
- **Plate 214** — undated, unnumbered; the vow, drawn from memory; kept by Pascual with John's permission. *John can redraw it at will and said so.*
- **Two file sheets** (18th, 19th May) — weight up nine pounds; water clear; *very lively, wd not keep still*. Travelled in Pascual's shirt through May and early June, then put away.
- **Plates 209 & 212** — in the Colonial Office quarterly fisheries return. Retrievable by written application, which Hester would hear about within the week.
- **The costing sheet** — 1984 underlined twice; *June 1955 — review* added in Pascual's hand; the boat before the house.
- **Spilsbury's letter to John** — Woods Hole, June 1954. Introductions offered, nothing asked. Still open.

## 7. People

- **Reggie Tar**, 34. 5'6", light, glasses. Eton, Cambridge, the government labs (4 years, adjoining rooms to Spilsbury), the tables in 1950, the Martin for 2 years.
  - *On the defect:* he doesn't leave results alone because he doesn't want to, not because he can't. He held publication as a live option and offered it. Appetite, and he knows it.
  - *On asking:* "I cannot ask anybody for anything" is what he **believes about himself**, said at half past ten at night, and it is not true. He asks for a great deal, particularly in bed, and gets it. Write the belief; never write it as fact.
- **Pascual Pobil**, 41. Just shy of six feet, strongly built, reading glasses only, winch scar under the left collarbone. Vet 19 years, at the Martin ~15 months. *He can destroy things. He would rather not, and calls it a defect, which is a man being hard on himself.*
- **Jean Leslie**, 29. Second author. Has the salt ponds with a budget line and Charles's fish counts. Bought them 90 minutes and will never mention it.
- **John Clarke**, 24. Lied to Spilsbury from a standing start. Has the plate sequence as primary record, named the calf, drew the portrait for Shropshire. Told them never to thank him. Holds an open door out.
- **Hester Bevan** — administrator; bonded with **Tom** 41 years ago and lost him; built the Martin looking for someone like Charles. Has the notebook.
- **Colonel Bevan** — political shield. Eleven words at the pen rail. Not young.
- **Alec Spilsbury** — physiologist/pathologist. An evening in 1946 neither has mentioned. Told Reggie on the jetty he'd think about it for ten years and come back, or somebody worse would.
- **Nicholson** — arrested Camberwell c. 1950, lost his post and his wife, now in Southport doing something with paint.
- **Victoria Cholmondeley** and her sister **Lettice**, Shrewsbury.
- **Animals**: Alpha (dead, pneumonia, pre-Charles). Beta/**Monty** (gone; alive). Gamma (female, adult, +31 lb by July, fat). Delta/**Chumley** (juvenile male, named by John 24 May 1954).
