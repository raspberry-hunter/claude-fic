# Account Rendered

*Account Rendered* has a main line and a tree of variants. The a-line is the story as it runs. The b-line forks from it at the last line of *Without Prejudice*, and every other line forks from a point inside b, or inside another fork. You read a variant by following the lines above it as far as the fork, then changing folders. A chapter's number is its place in the story, so 5c stands where 5b would. Where a folder holds alternatives rather than a sequence, its line says so.

The chapter lists below are generated by `mkcontents.sh` and rewritten every time it runs. Everything else in this note is left alone.

## a-line: Account Rendered

The main line. In February 1957 a letter comes from Bedford Row, and Frank Nicholson in Southport is asked a question nobody has asked him since 1950. Hester objects to a member of her staff being questioned, the inspector asks one question, and somebody gives notice. A year of letters follows between the Institute and Ålesund, none of them saying anything that could not be read aloud at the long table. In March 1958 the coastal steamer is four minutes early. It ends that August at Frank's house off Lord Street: fish pie for four, a handshake, a savings book, and the only apology in the account.

**Read:** straight through. Every other line branches off this one.

## e-line: Alec lives; Reggie and Pascual

Forks from *Yours Faithfully* (3b), before the vet puts his hand in the wrong bag of fish. Alec is alive in Ålesund and Pascual never marries Marguerite. In *Interest Accrued*, Pascual, who got over something in 1956, notices a thing he didn't ask to notice. After that the folder holds **two alternative continuations**. Read one or the other:

- **4e–5e, the short way.** On the step in November 1967 Reggie sees what has been in front of him for fifteen years, and Jean tells him the lamp is his to put out, not Pascual's. In 1970 Alec writes from London expecting no answer, and gets one by return.
- **1004e–1008e, the long way.** Across 1965 and 1966 the gap on the step closes a few inches at a time. Reggie tells Alec in an acknowledgement in the form with one line added, sees him in Regent's Park that September, and comes home, and Pascual puts the lamp out. These five carry `order:` 4e–8e, which is why the numbers below repeat.

**Read:** 1, 2b, *Yours Faithfully* as far as the vet, *Interest Accrued*, then one of the two runs.

## f-line: Alec dies; Reggie and Pascual

- *Night Safe* (9f-iii) is a variant of *On Demand*, and *At Sight* (9f-iv) a variant of *Night Safe*.

## g-line: Win

Follows d: *Brought Forward* picks up after *Carried Forward* and *Per Pro*. Having got over Alec, Reggie falls for Win, who keeps Harbour House at the Bay. Her husband Desmond comes back from Miami in 1973, with debts; the decree absolute comes through in 1974, and Reggie and Win marry that June. From there it's the family: Pearl from four to fifteen, Ike, Pearl at eighteen with a boy who plays like her grandfather, Pearl and Sammy's wedding at the Bay in 1998, and the letters to 2003. Reggie lives long enough to see Pearl grown.

*Foreign Exchange* (18g2) is an alternate to *In Credit*, which is in `NotUsed/` and stays out of the book.

**Read:** the d-line, then this folder.
